EDIRAA - ONE SINGLE VIDEO STORY + STORY VIEWERS & LIKES
=======================================================
HOW TO APPLY
1. Copy the src/ and supabase/ folders from this zip into your project root, choose "Replace".
   (APPLY-THIS.txt is not part of the app.)
2. Supabase -> SQL Editor -> run:
     supabase/migrations/20261020000000_story_single_video_views_likes.sql
   (needs the earlier migrations already run; safe to run more than once).
   !! Do this BEFORE posting a 26s story. Without it the database still rejects any
      single story longer than 15s, and the app will tell you to run this file.
3. npm run dev   (no new packages)

WHAT CHANGED
1. One video = one story. A 26s video with the 30s limit is now ONE 26s story (no 2 x 15s).
   The dialog says "Posted as 1 story." A video longer than the limit you pick is still rejected.
   Stories that were already posted in 15s parts keep playing as before.
2. Story viewers + likes
   - Watching someone else's story records you as a viewer (once per story).
   - Viewers get a heart button on the story. Tap again to unlike.
   - On YOUR story, the bottom shows eye + heart counts. Tap it to open the viewers list:
     who viewed (newest first) and a red heart next to everyone who liked it.
     The story pauses while the list is open.
   - If the SQL above has not been run, the eye/heart buttons are simply hidden and
     stories keep working.
