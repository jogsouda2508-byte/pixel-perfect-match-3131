Community easier to find (step 4 of the improvements list)

Apply steps 1 and 2 first (this edits community.tsx, which step 1 changed, and home.tsx).

1. Unzip into the ROOT of your project (same folder structure). Replace when asked.
2. Supabase > SQL Editor > paste and run:
     supabase/migrations/20261106000000_community_home.sql
   (needs 20261104000000_community.sql; safe to run twice).
   If you skip this, nothing breaks: the dot and the card just stay hidden.

What changes:
  - Home top bar (phones) and the desktop side menu: a small count on the Community icon =
    alerts and events from OTHER people posted since you last opened Community (9+ if more).
    Opening Community clears it.
  - Home feed: a one-line card under "What's on your mind?", tap opens the right tab:
      new alerts            "1 new alert near you"            (alert title underneath)
      events today          "2 events today near you"
      events this weekend   "2 events this weekend near you"  (next event + time underneath)
      active alerts         "3 active alerts near you"
      next event            "Next event near you"
      nothing yet           "See what's happening near you"
    "near you" = the area Community is set to; if it is set to everywhere it says "on Community".
  - /community?tab=alerts links now also work from inside the app (route accepts ?tab).
