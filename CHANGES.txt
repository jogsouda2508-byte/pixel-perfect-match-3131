EDIRAA - PRIVATE / PUBLIC ACCOUNTS + FOLLOW REQUESTS (and earlier updates)
=========================================================================
HOW TO APPLY
1. Unzip, copy src/ and supabase/ into your project root, choose "Replace".
2. Supabase -> SQL Editor -> run these IN ORDER (each is safe to run twice; skip any you already ran):
     20261019000000_post_location_tags.sql
     20261020000000_story_likes.sql
     20261021000000_private_accounts.sql      <-- NEW (run this BEFORE opening the app)
3. npm run dev   (no new packages)

HOW PRIVATE / PUBLIC WORKS
- Onboarding step 1 now asks "Who can see your posts?": Public (default) or Private. You can change it later in
  Settings & Privacy -> "Account privacy" switch.
- PUBLIC account: anyone can follow instantly (as before).
- PRIVATE account: tapping Follow sends a follow REQUEST (button shows "Requested"; tap again to cancel).
  The person only becomes a follower when the account owner accepts.
- A non-follower of a private account sees the profile header (photo, name, bio) with a lock card
  "This account is private". Their posts, videos, tagged posts, stories, likes and comments are hidden by the
  database itself (not just the screen). Follower / following lists are not opened for locked profiles.
- Example: you are public, your friend is private -> you cannot see her posts or stories until she accepts you.
- Notifications (Activity):
    * "X wants to follow you"  + Accept / Decline buttons        (to the private account)
    * "X accepted your follow request" + View profile             (to the requester)
    * "X started following you"                                    (after accepting / public follows)
  They also count in the red unread dot, and appear under the Follows tab and All.
- Declining is quiet (the requester is not told) and they may ask again. Switching a private account to public
  accepts everyone who was waiting. Existing followers stay when you go private.
