Community notifications (step 2 of the improvements list)

Apply step 1 (WhatsApp sharing) first if you have not: tapping a notification opens
/community?tab=alerts or ?tab=events, and that link handling comes from step 1.

1. Unzip into the ROOT of your project (same folder structure). Replace when asked.
2. Supabase > SQL Editor > paste and run:
     supabase/migrations/20261105000000_community_notifications.sql
   It needs the chat push migrations and the Community migration to be run already (it stops
   with a clear message if one is missing). Safe to run twice.
3. Nothing else to deploy: the send-push Edge Function is unchanged, and the chat push setup
   (SETUP-PUSH.md, steps 4 and 5) already covers this. To pause ALL push, including these:
     delete from private.push_config;

What people get (only people who turned on notifications and have a location in their profile):
  - a new alert in their area        "Power cut" + "Reported in Whitefield. Not confirmed yet."
  - that alert reaching 2 confirms   once; replaces the first notification, never a second one
  - a new event in their area        "Weekend cricket" + "Tomorrow, 7 PM . Ground 2"
  - an event they joined is cancelled
Each person can switch alerts and events off in Settings > Community notifications.
"Near" = the location in their profile (the same rule as the "Local" badge on answers).

Safety: a push problem never blocks a post, vote or cancel. A person's 4th alert or event within
an hour is saved but nobody is notified (spam guard). Big areas are sent in groups of 400.
