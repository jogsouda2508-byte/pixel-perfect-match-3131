ANDROID MIC FIX - how to apply
1. Unzip into your project ROOT and choose Replace (paths are src/... and scripts/...).
2. In the project root run:   node scripts/add-android-mic-permission.mjs
   (adds the RECORD_AUDIO permission to android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml; safe to run twice)
3. Then run:                  npx cap sync android
4. Rebuild and run the app from Android Studio. UNINSTALL the old app from the phone first.
5. Open a chat, hold the mic: Android shows 'Allow <app> to record audio?'. Tap Allow, then hold the mic again.
Also make sure you did NOT recreate src/routes/chat.id.tsx (only chat.$id.tsx must exist).
